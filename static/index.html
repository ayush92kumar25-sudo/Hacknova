🌍 5 Golden Rules of Zero-Waste Living

01

Refuse Single-Use

Carry a reusable canvas tote and stainless steel water bottle. Decline disposable plastic cutlery.

02

Rinse Before Binning

Milk pouches, tin cans, and sauce jars must be rinsed. Food contamination ruins entire recycling batches.

03

Segregate at Source

Maintain separate dual bins (Wet & Dry) inside kitchens. Once mixed, recovering dry recyclables is near impossible.

04

Compost Kitchen Scraps

Transform fruit peels and tea grounds into nutrient-rich compost for residential gardens and potted plants.
